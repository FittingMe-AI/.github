FittingMe brings online shopping closer to the fitting room. On the product page, shoppers get an answer to the two questions that hold them back — **“How does it look on me?”** and **“Is it the right size?”** — before they add to cart.

## What we build

| Product | What the shopper gets |
|---|---|
| **Virtual try-on** | The garment on themselves, from their own photo — not a generic avatar, not a model proxy. On mobile they pick a photo from their phone; on desktop, a QR code sends one from the phone. |
| **Size recommendation** | The recommended size, explained, with a fit score, before adding to cart. Three methods to choose from: enter a few measurements, describe body shape, or take photos. |

Both run as widgets on the retailer's product pages: FittingMe buttons open a drawer on the page, so the purchase journey stays as it is.

## Integration

- **One light script** — an asynchronous script tag and a container on the product page.
- **Fits your stack** — Shopify, WooCommerce, PrestaShop, Magento, Salesforce Commerce Cloud or a custom stack, as long as the platform allows the script on the product page.
- **Your catalogue, your size chart** — each product is linked to its catalogue reference; no rebuild of the purchase journey.
- **Live in a few days.**

## Privacy

- Temporary photo retention is explained to the shopper before they confirm.
- A confirmed photo may be kept for future try-ons in the same store and browser.
- Shoppers can delete it from the widget at any time; it is erased no later than 48 hours after their last try-on activity.
